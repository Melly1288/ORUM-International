ABOUT ORUM

Connecting supply with opportunity.

ORUM International is a Singapore-based FMCG sourcing and trading company connecting market demand with credible supply opportunities.

We work with buyers, distributors, importers, exporters, brand owners and supply partners to identify relevant products, sourcing solutions and commercial opportunities across markets.

Behind ORUM International is more than 16 years of hands-on FMCG commercial experience across Singapore and Southeast Asia, spanning food, consumer products, wine, spirits and beer. Experience with international brands, market development, distributor and retail networks, Route-to-Market and commercial strategy shapes our practical focus on product-market fit, credible partners and sustainable commercial potential.

The ORUM name is inspired by aurum, the Latin word for gold — reflecting our ambition to uphold a gold standard in partnerships and business dealings through integrity, commercial discipline and sustainable value.
